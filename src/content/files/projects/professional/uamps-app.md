# uamps-app

Enterprise software for the energy market: scheduling, billing, and market
operations for member utilities.

The defining constraint is that the requirements don't exist yet. This is a
new energy market, new for the third-party entities involved too, and
they're working it out as we go. The spec moves underneath you. You build
for what's decided this month knowing part of it changes next month.

Three developers, me leading. Weekly releases, new features in almost every
one.

**stack**: TypeScript, React, FastAPI, MongoDB
